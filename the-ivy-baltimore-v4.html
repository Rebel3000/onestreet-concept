The Ivy

Once a Mount Vernon mansion,
now a world-class hotel.

Gorgeous, romantic and welcoming, in the heart of Baltimore's most graceful neighborhood. Seventeen rooms and suites, charming public spaces, a small luxury spa and Magdalena, A Maryland Bistro. Take your coffee in the garden, choose a book from the library, try the piano or the billiards table. The property is yours.

Rooms & Suites

Seventeen,
no two alike.

Canopies, fireplaces
and limestone baths.

Every room in the mansion was furnished on its own terms: octagonal sitting rooms over the courtyard, a corner turret with a dining table, vaulted beams under the eaves. Bathrooms lean on deep soaking tubs, walk-in showers and heated limestone floors.
